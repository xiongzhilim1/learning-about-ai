# Portfolio

The language of Sean's portfolio-first site: who it speaks to, what it claims, and what counts as evidence.

## Language

**Encoded judgment**:
Expert judgment put into a form AI can use: opinionated flows, thinking primitives, product-depth skills, and the evals that check them. The pattern the positioning hangs on.
_Avoid_: Agents (as the umbrella term), automation, AI tools

**Agents**:
The searchable surface word for what encoded judgment makes work. Used in copy for findability, never as a claim of deploying autonomous agents at scale.

**Lead audience**:
The audience the home page's first screen is written for: people hiring for AI strategy, Deployment Strategist, and Applied AI roles.
_Avoid_: Target user, primary persona

**Secondary audiences**:
Paid-project clients (served by the same proof with a different call to action) and speaking bookers (served later, once footage and testimonials exist).

**Proof**:
Evidence a visitor can check that backs the positioning claim: case studies, frameworks, courses, artifacts, talks.
_Avoid_: Portfolio items, projects (as the umbrella term)

**Deployment roles**:
The target role family. "Deployed PM" is not a live title; the nearest real ones are Deployment Strategist, Deployment Lead, AI Deployment Manager, and Applied AI Strategist.
_Avoid_: Deployed PM (as a title)
